# UX improvement waiting list — 2026-07-01

Captured from a hands-on product review. Items **not** shipped in the 2026-07-01 bug-fix pass; prioritize as capacity allows.

## High impact

1. **Prominent passport scan on owner home** — `OwnerScanCard` is collapsed in an accordion; the claim flow is the growth loop. Make scan/claim a primary CTA beside “Add pet”.
2. **Shop onboarding checklist** — After signup/KYC: “Add pet → Log vaccines → Issue first passport” with progress on `/app`.
3. **Mobile bottom nav density** — Shop/facility bar has 7 items (Home, AI, Pets, Reminders, Notifications, Billing, Account). Consider a “More” menu or drop Billing from the bar.
4. **Feedback on mobile shop** — Desktop sidebar has Feedback; mobile header only on some breakpoints — add to mobile nav or overflow menu.

## Medium impact

5. **Actionable empty states** — “0 need attention” / “No reminders” should link to “Log observation” or “Set reminder”.
6. **Transfer tab discoverability** — Dashboard CTA when a pet is handover-ready: “Issue passport for Pearl”.
7. **Global pet search** — Extend shop search to dashboard (name, breed, microchip).
8. **Locale flash** — Brief EN→中文 flicker when `localStorage` differs from cookie on cold load (mitigated by hydration fix; full fix may need middleware or accept one refresh).
